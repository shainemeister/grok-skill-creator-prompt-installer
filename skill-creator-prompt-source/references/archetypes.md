# Skill Archetypes

## Minimal
Lightweight utility or thin wrapper. Very concise `SKILL.md` with minimal rules.

## Standard
Most common archetype. Clear purpose with balanced guidance. Good default for most domain skills.

## Orchestrator
Stateful, multi-step, or workflow-heavy skills. Stronger structure, safety considerations, and often benefits from helper scripts.

## Domain Expert
Skills that require deep domain knowledge. Heavy use of the `references/` folder for documentation, schemas, and examples.
